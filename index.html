03 — The Atelier

Eleven years
in a single drop.

Every BGS CORNER flacon begins as raw lead crystal, cut by hand over four days. The stopper is faceted separately — sixty-two facets, ground until the seal is airtight without adhesive.

Inside, a maceration of no fewer than six months. We release two hundred and forty bottles a year. When they are gone, they are gone.
